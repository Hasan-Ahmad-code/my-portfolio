About Me

Student · Researcher · Builder

I'm a CS student at Federal Urdu University, currently writing a survey paper on dynamic memory and test-time adaptation in large language models (DMTA-LLMs). My goal is to understand how AI systems can become more efficient, adaptive, and context-aware — without requiring expensive retraining.

I'm passionate about the gap between how humans use memory fluidly — and how language models currently handle it. This gap is where my research lives.

Beyond research, I work on HCI, VR/AR concepts, and enjoy explaining complex ideas through relatable, everyday analogies.
