Adaptive Context Reliance Control Final-year project

A system that dynamically controls how much a RAG pipeline relies on retrieved context under noisy retrieval conditions, to improve response accuracy. Built on a small dataset with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings; ten structured experiments across chunk size, embedding models, distance metrics, and noise injection — the key finding being that relevance is a continuous spectrum, and fixed thresholds are fragile.

Advanced to the Prescreening Judging Phase of the P@SHA ICT Awards 2026, Tertiary Students category.
